# TyMed Privacy Policy

Effective **[effective date]** · Applies to TyMed for Android

Published copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/3dDgZ3qApmgKxCY8vDHzLS

> **In short:** TyMed stores everything — your medications, schedules, and intake history — only on your device.
> Nothing is uploaded, synced, sold, or shared. There is no account and no server. Uninstalling the app permanently
> deletes your data.

## What TyMed is

TyMed is a medication reminder app for Android. It helps you track medications, schedule reminders, and log whether
a dose was taken or skipped. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Data we collect

**None, by default.** TyMed does not require an account, does not connect to a backend server, and does not
transmit your medication names, dosages, schedules, or intake history anywhere. All of that data is stored
exclusively in a local database on your device using Android's standard app storage, which only TyMed can access.

### Optional crash and error reporting

If enabled, TyMed may use [Sentry](https://sentry.io) to report app crashes and errors, to help us fix bugs. These
reports contain only technical diagnostic information — device model, OS version, and the error's stack trace — and
never include your medication names, dosages, schedules, or any other content you've entered into the app.

## Permissions TyMed requests

Android requires apps to declare the system permissions they use. Here's what TyMed requests and why:

| Permission | Why TyMed needs it |
|---|---|
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | To show dose reminders. |
| `USE_EXACT_ALARM` | To ring a dose alarm at the exact scheduled time, like a real alarm clock. |
| `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT` | To show the alarm screen even when your device is locked. |
| `VIBRATE`, `WAKE_LOCK` | To vibrate and keep the device awake while a dose alarm is ringing. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` | To keep the alarm sound playing until you respond. |

None of these permissions give TyMed — or us — access to data outside the app, and none are used to collect or
transmit information about you.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, because we don't collect any to begin with. TyMed
contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or trackers beyond the optional crash reporting described
above.

## Data deletion

Since all data lives only on your device, you're always in control of it. Deleting a medication in the app removes
its records immediately. Uninstalling TyMed permanently deletes all app data with it — we have no copy to retain,
because none ever left your device.

## Children's privacy

TyMed is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, note them in the app's
release notes.
