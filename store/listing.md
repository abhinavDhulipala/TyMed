Character limits are Google Play's actual field limits — paste these directly into Play Console.

## Title (≤30 characters)

```
TyMed: Pill Reminders
```

## Short description (≤80 characters)

```
Medication reminders that won't stop ringing until you take your dose.
```

## Full description (≤4000 characters)

```
TyMed is a medication reminder app built for one job: making sure you never miss a dose.

WON'T STOP UNTIL YOU RESPOND
Most reminder apps send a notification and hope you notice. TyMed rings — a real, full-screen
alarm with sound and vibration, just like your alarm clock, that keeps going until you mark
your dose taken. If you snooze, it rings again a few minutes later. And again. Until it's done.

TRACK YOUR ADHERENCE AT A GLANCE
A monthly calendar shows exactly how consistent you've been, with a simple ring on each day —
fuller and greener the more doses you took. Tap any day to see exactly what happened.

NEVER RUN OUT
Track pill counts per medication and get a heads-up when you're running low, before the bottle
is empty.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
TyMed doesn't require an account. There's no server, no sync, no ads, and nothing about your
medications ever leaves your phone. See our Privacy Policy for details.

FEATURES
• Full-screen, non-stop dose alarms — not just a notification
• Adjustable snooze/follow-up interval
• Multiple reminder times per medication
• Pill count and refill tracking
• Monthly adherence calendar with per-day history
• 100% local storage — no account required
```

## Category

Medical (primary) — Health & Fitness is a reasonable alternate if Medical requires additional
review documentation you're not ready to provide at launch.

## Content rating questionnaire notes

- No user-generated content, no violence, no mature themes.
- References medications/health, so expect the "Medical" or "References to drugs" style
  questions — answer honestly: the app helps users track their *own* prescribed/OTC medications,
  it does not sell, promote, or provide dosing advice for any substance.
